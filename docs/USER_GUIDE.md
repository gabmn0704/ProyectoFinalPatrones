# EpiSafe AI - User Guide

## Welcome to EpiSafe AI

EpiSafe AI is an intelligent system designed to help you manage your epilepsy by:
- 📊 **Track Health Factors**: Log daily information about sleep, stress, and medication
- 🔍 **Identify Patterns**: AI analyzes your data to find what triggers your seizures
- 📈 **Predict Risk**: Get daily risk scores based on your current health status
- 🚨 **Emergency Mode**: Record an event and open a prepared WhatsApp message to a trusted contact
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Care Coordination**: Share information with family and healthcare providers

## Tracking Your Health

### Daily Health Log

A **daily log** is your health snapshot for one day. Log once per day (or whenever you want).

**What to track:**

| Field | What it means | Example |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| **Date** | Date of entry | January 15, 2024 |
| **Sleep Hours** | How much you slept last night | 7.5 hours |
| **Stress Level** | How stressed you felt (1-10 scale) | 5 (moderate) |
| **Caffeine** | How much caffeine today (0-10 scale) | 3 (3 cups of coffee) |
| **Exercise** | How many minutes of exercise | 30 minutes |
| **Medication** | Did you take your medication? | Yes ✓ |
| **Notes** | Anything important to remember | "Felt good today" |

**How to add a daily log:**

```
1. Go to Dashboard
2. Click "Log Health Today" button
3. Fill in the form:
   📅 Date: [Today's date auto-filled]
   😴 Sleep: [Enter hours you slept]
   😟 Stress: [Slide scale 1-10]
   ☕ Caffeine: [Slide scale 0-10]
   🏃 Exercise: [Enter minutes]
   💊 Medication: [Toggle Yes/No]
   📝 Notes: [Enter any notes]
4. Click "Save" button
5. Confirmation: "Health log saved!"
```

**Examples:**

**Example 1: Good Day**
```
Date: January 15, 2024
Sleep: 8 hours
Stress: 3/10 (very low)
Caffeine: 1 (just 1 cup tea)
Exercise: 45 minutes
Medication: Yes ✓
Notes: "Great day! Felt very good."
```

**Example 2: Challenging Day**
```
Date: January 14, 2024
Sleep: 5 hours (not enough!)
Stress: 9/10 (very high)
Caffeine: 6 (lots of coffee to stay awake)
Exercise: 0 minutes
Medication: Yes ✓
Notes: "Stressful work day. Stayed up late."
```

**Why these metrics matter:**

- 🛏️ **Sleep**: Less sleep = higher seizure risk. Aim for 7-8 hours.
- 😟 **Stress**: High stress is a common trigger. Notice patterns.
- ☕ **Caffeine**: Too much may increase seizure risk (varies by person).
- 🏃 **Exercise**: Regular moderate exercise can reduce seizures.
- 💊 **Medication**: Missing doses increases seizure risk significantly.
- 📝 **Notes**: Help you remember what was different on certain days.

## Recording Seizures

### Emergency Mode

When a seizure happens, quickly record it using **Emergency Mode**.

**How to use Emergency Mode:**

```
1. As soon as seizure passes, go to app
2. Click 🚨 "EMERGENCY" button (red, hard to miss!)
3. App opens emergency form:

   🚨 EMERGENCY: Report Seizure
   
   Date: [Today auto-filled]
   Time: [Current time, you can edit]
   Duration: [How long seizure lasted]
   
   How long (minutes)? [Enter number]
   
   Severity: [Choose one]
   ☐ Mild    (brief, partial awareness)
   ☐ Moderate (moderate loss of awareness)
   ☑ Severe   (complete loss of awareness)
   
   What happened? [Describe the seizure]
   [Text area for description]
   
   Where were you? [Location]
   [Text field - e.g., "Home - Living room"]
   
   Did you go to hospital?
   ☐ No
   ☑ Yes
   
4. Click "Report Seizure" button
5. The event is recorded in your history
6. If a contact has an international phone number, tap "Open WhatsApp" and then tap Send to alert them
```

**Important Notes on Seizure Recording:**

- 📱 **Record as soon as possible** after seizure when details are fresh
- ⏱️ **Time**: Estimate if you're not sure - it's okay to be approximate
- ⏱️ **Duration**: How long the actual seizure lasted (not post-seizure confusion)
- 📝 **Description**: What do you remember? Any aura before? What you felt after?
- 📍 **Location**: Important for medical professionals to know
- 🏥 **Hospital**: Did you seek medical care? Helps track serious episodes

**What happens after you report:**

```
Immediate:
✓ Seizure recorded to your medical history
✓ WhatsApp opens a prepared message for the highest-priority contact with a valid phone number
✓ Timestamp recorded for doctor reference
✓ Confirmation message: "Seizure recorded"

Later:
→ You can view it in "Seizure History"
→ AI adds it to trigger analysis
→ Doctor can see it (if you shared access)
→ Helps identify patterns
```

## Understanding Your Data

### AI Insights

EpiSafe AI analyzes your data to find patterns:

**Example Insight #1: Sleep Pattern**
```
"You experienced 80% of seizures after sleeping
less than 5 hours the previous night."

What to do:
→ Prioritize 7-8 hours of sleep
→ Set bedtime reminders
→ Avoid staying up late
```

**Example Insight #2: Stress Connection**
```
"High stress (8+/10) preceded 3 of your last 4 seizures
with 2-8 hours delay."

What to do:
→ Practice stress reduction: meditation, yoga
→ Exercise regularly to manage stress
→ Talk to counselor or therapist
```

**Example Insight #3: Medication Timing**
```
"You missed medication twice last month.
Both times followed seizures within 24 hours."

What to do:
→ Set daily medication reminders
→ Use a pill organizer
→ Take medication at same time daily
```

### Risk Score

Every day, EpiSafe AI calculates your **Daily Risk Score**:

```
Risk Score: 65%

This means: 65% chance of a seizure today based on your data

Factors contributing to HIGH risk today:
• Sleep: 5 hours (you need 7-8)      [Weight: 40%]
• Stress: 8/10 (very high)            [Weight: 35%]
• Medication: Taken on time           [Weight: 15%]
• Exercise: None today                [Weight: 10%]

What you can do NOW to reduce risk:
→ Try to rest and recover tonight
→ Practice deep breathing (5 min)
→ Avoid caffeine after this afternoon
→ Check emergency contacts are reachable
```

**Understanding the risk:**
- 🟢 **0-20%**: Low risk - normal day
- 🟡 **20-50%**: Moderate risk - be cautious
- 🟠 **50-80%**: High risk - take precautions
- 🔴 **80-100%**: Very high risk - consider medical help

## Managing Contacts

### Emergency Contacts

Emergency contacts are trusted people you can reach when you need support.

**Why add emergency contacts:**
- They can get immediate help to you
- They stay informed about your health
- They can provide witness accounts
- You feel less alone

**Who to add:**
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Spouse/Partner
- 👨‍👩‍👦 Family member (parent, sibling)
- 👨‍⚕️ Doctor or neurologist
- 🏥 Trusted friend or neighbor

**How to add a contact:**

```
1. Go to Dashboard
2. Click ⚙️ "Settings"
3. Click "Emergency Contacts"
4. Click "+ Add Contact" button
5. Fill in:
   👤 Name: "Jane Doe"
   👥 Relationship: "Spouse"
   📱 Phone: "+52 55 1234 5678" (optional; use international format)
   📧 Email: "jane@example.com" (optional)
6. Click "Save Contact"
```

**What contacts receive:**

When you record an event, EpiSafe saves it to your history. For a contact with a phone number, the dialog offers a WhatsApp link to the highest-priority contact with a valid number. You must open WhatsApp and tap Send yourself; EpiSafe does not send messages automatically or contact emergency services. The prepared text does not include event notes or medical details.

Optional email alerts are available only when the server email provider is configured. WhatsApp is not SMS; the recipient needs WhatsApp, and the user must confirm sending in WhatsApp.

**Managing contacts:**
```
View all contacts:
→ Settings → Emergency Contacts

Update contact:
→ Click contact name
→ Edit phone/email
→ Toggle notifications on/off
→ Click "Update"

Remove contact:
→ Click contact name
→ Click "Remove Contact"
→ Confirm: "Yes, remove"
```

## Tips & Best Practices

### Logging Consistently

✅ **Best Practice**: Log every evening before bed

```
Timing: Set a reminder for 8:00 PM
Benefit: You remember the whole day clearly
Accuracy: Details are fresh
Habit: Makes it automatic routine
```

### Detailed Seizure Notes

✅ **Good Notes** (helpful for patterns):
```
"Woke up from seizure. Intense from start.
Post-ictal confusion lasted 30 min.
Bit tongue slightly.
Felt anxious before (possibly aura?)."
```

❌ **Vague Notes** (less helpful):
```
"Had a seizure"
"Bad day"
"Sick"
```

### Identifying Your Triggers

📊 **How AI finds your triggers:**

1. **You log your data** → Sleep, stress, caffeine, etc.
2. **You record seizures** → Date, time, severity, notes
3. **AI looks for patterns** → What was common before seizures?
4. **AI reports findings** → "You get seizures when X happens"

Example process:
```
Your data:
Jan 15: Sleep 5h, Stress 9/10 → Seizure (evening)
Jan 10: Sleep 5h, Stress 8/10 → Seizure (next morning)
Jan 5:  Sleep 5h, Stress 7/10 → Seizure (afternoon)
Jan 1:  Sleep 8h, Stress 4/10 → No seizure ✓

AI finding: "80% of seizures follow nights with
less than 5 hours of sleep."
```

### Managing Medication

💊 **Tips for staying compliant:**

1. **Set phone reminders**
   - Time of day you take medication
   - Repeat daily at same time

2. **Use pill organizer**
   - Fill weekly on Sunday
   - Easy to see if you missed a dose

3. **Pair with routine**
   - Take with breakfast
   - Take before bed
   - Create habit so you don't forget

4. **Track in app**
   - Mark "medication taken" each day
   - AI tracks your compliance
   - Pattern matching includes medication status

### Stress Management

When risk score is high due to stress:

**Immediate (5-10 min)**:
- Deep breathing: 5 minutes
- Progressive muscle relaxation
- Short walk or light movement
- Listen to calming music

**Daily**:
- 20 minutes meditation/yoga
- Regular exercise (30 min, 3x/week)
- Journaling
- Time in nature

**Long-term**:
- Talk to therapist/counselor
- Join support group (for people with epilepsy)
- Stress management classes
- Doctor evaluation if persistent

### Sleep Hygiene

Since sleep is a major trigger for many:

**Good sleep habits:**
- ✅ Consistent bedtime (even weekends)
- ✅ 7-8 hours target
- ✅ No screens 1 hour before bed
- ✅ Cool, dark, quiet room
- ✅ Avoid caffeine after 2 PM
- ✅ Exercise during day (not evening)

**What to avoid:**
- ❌ Sleeping <5 hours (major risk!)
- ❌ Erratic sleep schedule
- ❌ Heavy meals right before bed
- ❌ Alcohol in evening
- ❌ Stress right before bed

### Understanding When to Seek Help

**See a doctor immediately if:**
- 🚨 Frequent or increasing seizures
- 🚨 Seizures lasting >5 minutes
- 🚨 Status epilepticus (multiple seizures without waking)
- 🚨 Injury during seizure
- 🚨 Difficulty breathing
- 🚨 Fever after seizure

**Schedule checkup if:**
- 📋 Side effects from medication
- 📋 Medication doesn't seem to be working
- 📋 Concerned about medication change
- 📋 Questions about your seizures

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How often should I log my health?**
A: At least once daily. Every evening is ideal so you remember the whole day.

**Q: Can I edit old logs?**
A: Yes! Click any past log to edit it.

**Q: What if I forget to log for a few days?**
A: Just log whenever you remember. Even partial data helps.

**Q: Will my data be deleted if I stop logging?**
A: No! Your data stays until you delete your account.

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Yes! Only you can see your data. Emergency contacts only see seizure alerts.

**Q: Can I share with my doctor?**
A: Not yet in this version, but it's coming! You can manually screenshot/export for now.

**Q: What if I have a seizure and can't use the app?**
A: EpiSafe cannot notify contacts unless someone opens the app and confirms the message in WhatsApp. Call local emergency services for immediate danger, and use your clinician-provided emergency plan.

**Q: Can I delete a log or seizure?**
A: Yes, but we keep a backup for medical records. Contact support if you need help.
